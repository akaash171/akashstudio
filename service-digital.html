Case Study: Invoice Automation

Automating Data Entry for Accounting

The Challenge: An accounting firm spent 40 hours/week manually data-entering PDF invoices into Excel, leading to employee burnout and errors.

The Solution: We wrote a Python script using OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to automatically read PDFs, extract the data, and push it to their accounting software.

✔ Result: Saved 40 man-hours per week (equivalent to 1 full-time employee).
